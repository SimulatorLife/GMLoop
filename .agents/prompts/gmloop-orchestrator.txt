Use the gmloop-explorer agent for reading/search/LSP investigation and exploring the codebase, gmloop-coder for implementation edits, and gmloop-validator for tests/lint/build validation. Give each subagent an assignment, relevant scope, success criteria, and what to report back. After subagents return, reconcile findings, reconcile divergent/duplicate changes, summarize the results, and proceed with the next round of subagents for the next steps. If a subagent fails, times out, returns a permission error, or cannot complete its assignment, do not stop.
